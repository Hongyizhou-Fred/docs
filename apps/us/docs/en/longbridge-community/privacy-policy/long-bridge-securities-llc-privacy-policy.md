Long Bridge Securities LLC and its affiliates are collectively referred to in this document as “Longbridge,” “we,” “our,” or “us.”

### **1\. How we collect and use your personal information**

When you use our products and/or services, your personal information may be collected and used in the following scenarios:

**a.** Information that you provide when you create an account.

When you choose to create a Longbridge account with your email address or mobile phone number, we will collect this kind of contact information. We may send emails, text messages, or push notifications to you. You may follow the relevant notice to unsubscribe related services.

When you register Longbridge with your existing Google account, Facebook account or other third-party accounts, we will obtain your personal information from the third party, such as your images, your nickname or username.

If you do not have a Longbridge account or use Longbridge without a login, you will not be regarded as a client of Longbridge. There will be no business relationship between you and Longbridge when you use Longbridge without a login.

You can also add or link your mobile phone numbers, email addresses, and third-party accounts to your existing Longbridge account. We will collect this additional information so you can log in to Longbridge using those credentials.

**b.** When you use our Services, we may automatically collect and store your usage information of Longbridge products and services. This kind of usage information includes but is not limited to how you access our Services, your actions on the Services, including your interactions with others on the Services, photos or media you upload to the Services, and other content you provide (“Usage Information”).

**c.** We will only access your device camera and photo album with your permission when you want to upload photos onto the Longbridge help center, update your account profile image, or post photos in the Longbridge community.

**d.** Longbridge also collects certain categories of Sensitive Personal Information—such as government identification numbers and financial account information. Sensitive Personal Information also includes AI interaction data—such as queries, prompts, and conversation logs you generate through use of the AI Financial Agent and AI Non-Financial Agent—which may contain information about your assets, financial situation, investment goals, and personal circumstances. This data is processed as described in the Automated Analysis and Artificial Intelligence section below and is primarily for identity verification, regulatory compliance, fraud prevention, and servicing your account.

**e.** We will only access your device microphone with your permission when you want to use a voice command to search stocks or place a trade.

**f.** We will only access your device calendar with your permission when you want to add alerts or economic data to your calendars.

**g.** Due to anti-money laundering and customer identification obligations, and in accordance with applicable U.S. laws and regulations, when you apply for a trading account you will be required to provide certain identifying information, including your full legal name, date of birth, U.S. residential address, email address, and a government-issued identification number. We may also require you to upload a copy of a valid government-issued photo identification. Longbridge currently opens accounts only for U.S. residents.

### **5\. How we protect your Information**

We have physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards that comply with federal regulations to protect your personal information. We take all reasonable security measures to protect your information against loss, improper use, unauthorized access or disclosure. We will work within the reasonable security standards and use a variety of security measures to protect the security of information. We have established a specialized management, process and organization system to ensure information security. For example, we strictly restrict the employees who can access information, requiring them to provide unique authentication to access information and comply with their confidentiality obligations and conduct audits.

We have established an emergency response plan. If there is a security breach involving the personal information of our users, we will activate the emergency response plan to prevent the expansion of such security incident and take appropriate measures to inform you. In the event of a cybersecurity incident that results in the unauthorized access, use, or disclosure of customer information, we will promptly investigate the matter and take appropriate remedial action. Customers whose information may have been affected will be notified in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. Such notification may include details regarding the nature of the incident, the types of information involved, steps we have taken to address the issue, and recommended actions customers can take to protect themselves.

Longbridge will notify affected individuals of any unauthorized access to customer information as required under the amended SEC Regulation S-P, generally within 30 days of discovery, unless law enforcement requests a delay or applicable state law requires a shorter notification period. New York residents should be aware that cybersecurity incidents may be reported to the New York Department of Financial Services within 72 hours where required by the DFS Cybersecurity Regulation (23 NYCRR 500). Notification timelines for other states vary; in all cases Longbridge will comply with the most stringent applicable requirement.

Longbridge also maintains and enforces a written Identity Theft Prevention Program in accordance with SEC Regulation S-ID.

References to “CCPA” in this section include the CCPA as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (“CPRA”). Specifically:

**a.** CalFIPA Affiliate Opt-In — We will not share your nonpublic personal financial information with LBAI or any other affiliated company for marketing purposes without your prior affirmative consent (opt-in).

**b.** Automated Decision-Making Opt-Out — To the extent required by applicable California law and regulations, you have the right to opt out of automated decision-making, including profiling that produces significant legal or financial effects, such as AI-generated investment recommendations. To exercise this right, contact support@longbridge.com.

**c.** AI Interaction Data — Queries and conversation logs generated through your use of our AI agents are collected and processed solely for the purposes described in the Automated Analysis section and are not sold to third parties.

Subject to certain limitations and exceptions, the CCPA provides California residents the following rights:

1.  The ability to request a covered business disclose the detailed personal information we collect;
2.  The opt-out of the sale of personal information, if applicable. We do not sell personal information;
3.  The option to control and delete personal information in accordance with CA law and our regulatory obligations. Longbridge gives all users the option to delete cookies from visits to our products and websites. To do so, please contact our customer services at support@longbridge.com.
4.  Not be discriminated against for exercising rights they have under CCPA.

In accordance with California law, we will not share information we collect about you with nonaffiliates except with your consent, as allowed by law or as required by our regulators. For example, we may share information with your consent or to service your accounts. Among our affiliates, we will limit information sharing to the extent required by California law.

For exercising CCPA specific rights, please contact our customer support team at: support@longbridge.com. We may verify your request by asking you to provide information that matches information we have on file about you. You can also designate an authorized agent to exercise these rights on your behalf, but we will require proof that the person is authorized to act on your behalf and may also still ask you to verify your identity with us directly.

### **12\. Automated Analysis and Artificial Intelligence**

Longbridge uses automated systems, including artificial intelligence and algorithmic tools, to analyze customer information for the following purposes: (a) fraud detection and prevention; (b) regulatory compliance, including anti-money laundering and customer identification; (c) investment analytics and portfolio analysis; (d) generating investment recommendations subject to Regulation Best Interest (“Reg BI”); (e) personalized platform insights; and (f) improving platform functionality and AI model performance. These tools analyze account activity, transaction data, investment profile information, and AI interaction data (including queries and conversation logs generated through your use of the AI Financial Agent and AI Non-Financial Agent). Longbridge’s affiliated technology provider, LBAI, operates the AI infrastructure and may access customer data solely for these purposes. Longbridge does not sell customer information and does not use automated systems to make investment decisions on behalf of customers without their direction or consent. Records of AI-generated investment recommendations, including the customer profile data used to generate each recommendation, are retained as required by applicable FINRA and SEC recordkeeping rules.

### **13\. AI Financial Agent and AI Non-Financial Agent**

The Longbridge platform provides two AI-powered conversational tools: (1) the AI Financial Agent, which provides investment-related information, portfolio analysis, market data, and, where applicable, investment recommendations subject to Regulation Best Interest; and (2) the AI Non-Financial Agent, which provides general, non-investment-advice-related assistance. These tools analyze your queries, investment profile, account activity, and other account context to generate responses. When the AI Financial Agent provides an investment recommendation, it uses your current investment profile (including risk tolerance, investment objectives, time horizon, and financial situation) and available market data to generate a recommendation. A record of each recommendation and the profile data used is retained as required by applicable law. AI-generated responses, including investment recommendations, may be incomplete or inaccurate and should not be relied upon as the sole basis for investment decisions. Securities recommendations, subject to Regulation Best Interest, generated by the AI Financial Agent are made based on the information available in your investment profile at the time of recommendation; they do not constitute ongoing investment advice and your account is not monitored on an ongoing basis for continuing risk. Customers in Colorado and other applicable states have the right to opt out of automated decision-making for consequential decisions and to request human review of AI-generated investment recommendations. However, having a human review of an AI-generated investment recommendation specific to your account is not a service offered by Longbridge. If you choose to opt out of AI-generated recommendations and other automated decision-making functions, you should consider whether an account at Longbridge will meet your expectations. To exercise this right, contact us at support@longbridge.com.
