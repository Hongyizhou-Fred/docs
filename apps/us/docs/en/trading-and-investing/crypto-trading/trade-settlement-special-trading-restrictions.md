_Crypto services on the Longbridge Platform are provided by Bakkt Crypto Solutions_

**What is the settlement cycle for crypto trades?**

Crypto trades on Bakkt settle in real-time.

Sale proceeds from your crypto sale are immediately added to your buying power (_available to your_ _Bakkt crypto account and your Longbridge Securities LLC brokerage account_). However, they are not available for withdrawal until they are reflected in the **Cash Available to Withdraw** balance in your brokerage account on T+1 of each business day morning.  Crypto trades executed before 8:15PM ET (approximately) each business day are considered trade date (T).

**Is short selling of crypto supported?**

No, Bakkt does not support short selling or margin trading for cryptocurrencies.
