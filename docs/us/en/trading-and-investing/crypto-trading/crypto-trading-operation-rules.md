**What are the trading hours for crypto?**

Crypto trading is available **24/7/365**, meaning you can trade crypto at any time, any day.  However, there are system maintenance windows during which trading will be temporarily unavailable. -   Monthly maintenance window:
    _First Tuesday of every month between the hours of **02:00AM – 05:00AM ET**. (Note that this may or not occur every month. Event notification will be provided on the Longbridge Platform.)_

**What type of orders are available on Bakkt?**

**Market Order:** 

Executes immediately at the current market price.

**Limit Order:**

Will only be executed at a specified price. (Limit orders are good for up to 90 days).

**Stop Limit Order:**

Executes (becomes a limit order) when the stop price is reached.

**What are the trading limits on Bakkt?**

Minimum order amount: **$1.00**

Maximum per order (buy or sell): **$100,000** 

Maximum total of pending orders (both buy and sell orders combined): **$200,000**

Smallest tradeable quantity for most of the coins: **0.00000001** (8 decimal points)
