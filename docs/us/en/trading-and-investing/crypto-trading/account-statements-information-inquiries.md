_Crypto services on the Longbridge Platform are provided by Bakkt Crypto Solutions_

**Will my cryptocurrency positions be on the same brokerage account statements for my securities positions?**

No, your cryptocurrency position will not appear on the Longbridge Securities LLC brokerage account statements.  Note that your Bakkt crypto account is separate from your brokerage account with Longbridge Securities LLC.

Although Bakkt sends email trade confirmation at the end of each trade day (approximately after 8:15PM ET), it does not currently provide account statements.

You can, however, view your Bakkt crypto account details through the Longbridge Platform.
