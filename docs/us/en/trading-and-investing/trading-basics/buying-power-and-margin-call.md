## **Buying power** 

Buying power is the amount of money you can use to purchase stocks, options, or crypto.

## **1\. Overnight Buying Power**

Overnight buying power = the lesser of (2× margin excess) and the greater of (2× SMA, 2× Reg-T excess)

Overnight buying power is the amount available to purchase securities intended to be held overnight. After liquidating an overnight position, overnight buying power is replenished by the sale proceeds, adjusted based on the maintenance requirement of the position sold.

## **2\. Day Trad****e** **Buying Power**

Day trade buying power = 4× your margin excess

Profits from day trades are added to your cash balance immediately but do not increase day trade buying power intraday. These profits will be reflected in day trade buying power on the next trading day. If you begin the day by liquidating positions held overnight, day trade buying power is replenished based on the lower of the previous day’s closing price or the sale price.

## **3\. Option** **Buying Power**

Option buying power = your available cash

Option buying power is only available after you enable options trading.

## **4\. Crypto Buying Power**

Crypto buying power is calculated based on your available cash, pending settlements, and the margin or collateral currently reserved for other positions.

Crypto buying power is only available after you enable crypto trading.

## **Margin call**

## **1\. Required** **Maintenance Call**

You may receive a required maintenance call when margin equity falls below maintenance requirement. When you trigger an required maintenance call, you can only close positions. Please deposit funds or reduce positions to restore margin excess.

## **2\. Reg-T Call**

You may receive a Reg-T call when Reg-T excess ≤ 0 and SMA ≤ 0. Liquidating to meet a Reg T call may result in a violation. To avoid a violation, please add funds by the deadline.

Ongoing violations within a 12-month period will result in the following:

-   3rd violation: Overnight BP restriction
-   4th violation: 90-day close only restriction

## **3\. Equity Maintenance Call**

If you flagged as PDT, you may receive an equity maintenance call when margin equity falls below $25,000. When you trigger an equity maintenance call, any new day trade will trigger a 90-day close only restriction.

What is PDT？

The full name of PDT is Pattern Day Trader. Executing 4 day trades within 5 business days will mark your account as a Pattern Day Trader. Once marked as a PDT, you are eligible for a one-time reset to remove this mark. Please contact customer service to apply for this waiver.

## **4\. Day-Trade Call**

You may receive a day trade call when day trade buying power exhausted. You should deposit funds by the deadline to cover the deficiency.

Ongoing violations within a 12\-month period will result in the following:

-   1st violation: Day trade buying power is limited to your MME for 90 days. During this period, selling positions will not release additional buying power.
-   2nd violation: Account restricted to close only for 90 days.

**5\. Concentration Margin Call** 

You may receive a concentration margin call when your account holds an overly concentrated position in one or more stocks. You should deposit funds or reduce your concentrated position before the deadline.

**Trigger conditions** — all three must be met:

-   Enable margin investing
-   Cash debit balance exceeds $100,000
-   CM Excess < 0 (Concentration Requirement > Margin Equity)

**How CM Excess is calculated:**

CM Excess = Margin Equity − Total Concentration Requirement

The Concentration Requirement for each stock is based on its CM Ratio (single stock market value ÷ total marginable holdings market value):

| CM Ratio | Concentration Requirement % |
| --- | --- |
| < 25% | Standard maintenance margin rate |
| ≥ 25% and < 35% | Higher of 35% or standard maintenance margin rate |
| ≥ 35% and ≤ 50% | Higher of 40% or standard maintenance margin rate |
| \> 50% | Higher of 50% or standard maintenance margin rate |

Note: ETFs and non-marginable stocks are excluded from this calculation.

 Ongoing violations will result in the following:

-   Overdue CM Call: Account restricted to close only until resolved.
